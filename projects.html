Soccer Scout

Our team project for HelloHack 2026. Search for a professional player, see a data-driven breakdown of how they play, get a training plan to build the same abilities, then practise in front of a live coach.

  • StatsBomb Open Data: 930 matches preloaded into per-90 stats and percentiles for 6,567 player-seasons
  • Card ratings are measured, estimated from the player's position group, or marked unavailable
  • Training plans target your two or three biggest gaps and end with a day-7 re-test
  • Live coach runs pose tracking and ball detection in the browser and gives spoken hints

Reel Cards

A personal iPhone web app that reads the French text on screen in an Instagram reel (or a screenshot), picks out the vocabulary and turns it into spaced-repetition flashcards. No paid APIs, no LLMs, no accounts.

  • The server pulls a frame every 2 seconds with ffmpeg and reads the French captions with Tesseract OCR
  • Words are reduced to their dictionary form (allait → aller) with meaning and gender from bundled open dictionaries
  • Cards live on the phone and review offline with SM-2 (Again, Hard, Good, Easy), with Anki CSV export
  • Learn tab: about 4,400 grammar questions from A1 to C1, 2,500 vocab words by level, and conjugations for about 7,200 verbs

Soccer Scout

Our team project for HelloHack 2026. Search for a professional player, see a data-driven breakdown of how they play, get a training plan to build the same abilities, then practise in front of a live coach.

  • StatsBomb Open Data: 930 matches preloaded into per-90 stats and percentiles for 6,567 player-seasons
  • Card ratings are measured, estimated from the player's position group, or marked unavailable
  • Training plans target your two or three biggest gaps and end with a day-7 re-test
  • Live coach runs pose tracking and ball detection in the browser and gives spoken hints

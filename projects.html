Daily (Life Tracker)

A productivity tracker built around time awareness, recurring tasks and daily routines. It answers one question: do you have enough time today?

  • Tabs of tasks with a duration and a schedule: daily, weekdays, weekends, a set day or one time
  • Morning, midday and evening routines that reset every day
  • A green, yellow or red status compares the time left in your day with what you've planned
  • Every task logs itself as done or missed at midnight, with weekly, monthly and yearly charts

Reel Cards

A personal iPhone web app that reads the French text on screen in an Instagram reel or a screenshot, picks out the vocabulary and turns it into spaced-repetition flashcards. No paid APIs, no AI models and no accounts.

  • The server pulls a frame every 2 seconds with ffmpeg and reads the French captions with Tesseract OCR
  • Words become their dictionary form (allait becomes aller) with meaning and gender from bundled open dictionaries
  • Cards live on the phone and review offline with SM-2 (Again, Hard, Good, Easy), with an Anki export
  • A Learn tab with about 4,400 grammar questions from A1 to C1, 2,500 vocab words by level, and conjugations for about 7,200 verbs

HelloHack 2026

Soccer Scout

Our team project for HelloHack 2026. Search for a professional player, see a data-driven breakdown of how they play, get a training plan to build the same abilities, then practise in front of a live coach.

  • StatsBomb Open Data: 930 matches preloaded into per-90 stats and percentiles for 6,567 player-seasons
  • Card ratings are measured, estimated from the player's position group, or marked unavailable
  • Training plans target your two or three biggest gaps and end with a day-7 re-test
  • The live coach runs pose tracking and ball detection in the browser and gives spoken hints

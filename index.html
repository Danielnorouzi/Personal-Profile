Reel Cards

An iPhone web app that reads the French captions in an Instagram reel or screenshot and turns the vocabulary into flashcards.

  • Pulls a frame every 2 seconds and reads the French on screen with OCR
  • Turns each word into its dictionary form (allait becomes aller) with meaning and gender
  • Reviews work offline with spaced repetition, plus grammar quizzes and a conjugation search for about 7,200 verbs

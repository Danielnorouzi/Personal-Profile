03 No paid APIs
Node.js·Tesseract OCR·ffmpeg·PWA

Reel Cards

An iPhone web app that reads the French captions in an Instagram reel or screenshot and turns the vocabulary into spaced-repetition flashcards.

  • ffmpeg pulls a frame every 2 seconds and Tesseract reads the French on screen
  • Words become their dictionary form (allait → aller) with meaning and gender from open dictionaries
  • Offline reviews with SM-2, plus grammar quizzes, vocab decks by level and a conjugation search for about 7,200 verbs

Reel Cards

An iPhone web app that reads the French captions in an Instagram reel or screenshot and turns the vocabulary into flashcards.

  • Reads the French on screen with OCR, one frame every 2 seconds
  • Turns each word into its dictionary form, so allait becomes aller
  • Reviews work offline with spaced repetition, plus grammar quizzes and about 7,200 verb conjugations
